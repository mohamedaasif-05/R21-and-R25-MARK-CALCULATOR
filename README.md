# 🎓 R21 & R25 Marks Calculator

A simple, responsive, and user-friendly **Internal–External Pass Mark Calculator** designed for students following R21 and R25 academic regulations.

This web-based calculator helps students determine the minimum external examination marks required to satisfy the overall passing criteria based on their internal marks and Lab ESE performance.

## 🚀 Live Features

- Supports both **R21 and R25 Regulations**.
- Calculates minimum external marks required.
- Supports multiple subject categories.
- Automatically calculates theory and laboratory contributions.
- Dynamically updates calculations when marks are entered.
- Validates input marks to prevent incorrect calculations.
- Displays whether both passing conditions are satisfied.
- Responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- No installation or additional dependencies required.

## 📚 Supported Regulations

### R21 Regulation

| Subject Category | Internal | External | Theory Weightage | Lab Weightage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pure Theory | 40 | 60 | 100% | - |
| 2-0-2 | 50 | 50 | 50% | 50% |
| 3-0-2 | 50 | 50 | 70% | 30% |

### R25 Regulation

| Subject Category | Internal | External | Theory Weightage | Lab Weightage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pure Theory | 40 | 60 | 100% | - |
| 3-0-4 | 50 | 50 | 80% | 20% |

## 📊 Passing Criteria

The calculator considers two passing conditions:

**1. Overall Aggregate – 50%**

Internal marks + External marks must be at least 50 out of 100.

**2. External ESE Aggregate – 45%**

The combined external examination performance must be at least 45%.

For integrated subjects, the external ESE aggregate is calculated using the applicable theory and laboratory weightages.

## 💻 How to Use

1. Open the calculator.
2. Select your regulation (R21 or R25).
3. Select your subject category.
4. Enter your internal marks.
5. For integrated subjects, enter your Lab ESE marks.
6. The calculator automatically displays the required theory/external marks.
7. Check the result to see whether both passing conditions are satisfied.
